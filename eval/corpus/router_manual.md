# Quick Start Guide – WR-300 Wireless Router

## Resetting the device

To restore factory defaults, press and hold the reset button on the back for 10 seconds until the power LED blinks.

## Default credentials

The default network name (SSID) and WiFi password are printed on the label on the bottom of the router.

## Firmware

Open http://192.168.0.1 in your browser and choose "Firmware update".
